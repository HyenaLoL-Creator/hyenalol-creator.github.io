Biology -

Appearance :

SheFurrs are: furry-based, canine-based, predatory, combining fur + skin + cartilage structures, mamals + light xenomorphic, and femininity. The layering goes: skin first - fur, and some cartilages in places and pieces. SheFurrs have many different colorations, but the canonical coloration is: darkish in stripes with weaky gray on chest, orange biolumen and eyes, white fingertips on paws.

Muzzle & Mouth :

SheFurrs have a mouth shaped like a full beak made of two jaws - meaning SheFurrs do not have teeth inside the mouth, but have them as part of the mouth structure. They have sharp predator fangs, and a snake-demonic tongue: long, flexible, and split into two like snake's, also tongue and mouth are glowing orange biolumen. They have a nose on the muzzle.

Eyes :

Total 5 eyes. Two basic normal-sized eyes, two smaller eyes slightlly above the main pair, one large central eye on the forehead - shaped like a Romb, but the Romb-eye is basically closed except the queens and princesses. Eyelashes present, but no brows. Four triangle little orange biolumen dots under main eyes. blinking is semi-rare, this more like optical refresh + light cleaning. Canon eye color: orange with biolumen.

The pupils of SheFurrs, canonically - are two triangular shapes: one in the place of the pupil, and the second extending from it as it's continuation. The Romb-eye pupil is hexagonal canonically.

Ears :

Sharp canine-furry ears.

Antennae :

On the head goes directly from brain - long insect-like feelers, but more like a feather's structure. Antennae glows orange biolumen. Feather-structured, not insect-structured, extremely elastic, directly connected to the brain, transmit sensory and hive-mind data (The antennae is the real language of SheFur by resonance), and are both a strength and a vulnerability. Elites / princesses / psyonics / gamergates / queens - are have an extra horn-antennae and the princesses & queens have more extra horn-antennaes.

Arms & Hands :

Two main arms with five fingers - each finger has ending in sharp claws. Two smaller secondary arms below the main pair, near the chest with five fingers - each also clawed. All fingers of paws and legs have white fingertips, like Furry paws.

Elites / psyonics - have extra additional two arms near shoulders on back.

Wings :

Some castes - scout / princess - have wings like hawk-wasp's on back.

Tail :

Long, spiked, sharp. Has 6 orange biolumen dots on the main spike.

Body :

Light, but defined musculature. Two strong predator legs with digitigrade furry base with 4 orange biolumen dots per each leg. Orange tongue. Fur / cartilage / skin - combination. 4 orange biolumen dots on stomach. Cartilaginous structures such as: tail, antennae, spine, claws, and "horn-antennae" - (In some specific castes) - are not rigid, but soft. However, in any dangerous situation or under any threat - these structures immediately becomes hardened.

Chest :

A visible scar-like line in the center - heart-scar - this is where the heart is located, the heart can be seen glowing through this line with bright orange biolumen. Also on chest - SheFurrs have 2 milk glands.

Hair :

They have feminine hair on the head, canonical dark colors. They don't care much about grooming for hair, so hairstyles are often: messy, short, long, unkempt.

Anatomy -

Nervous System & Systems :

Full spinal cord from head to tail, porous bone channels protect neural tissue. They feel pain, but have higher pain thresholds. Brain is whole, not divided into sectors and has 3d-hexagonal structure, and has brighty orange biolumen. Antennae convert neural signals into long-range sensory transmissions. The nerve system is brighty orange biolumen. They have nerve system all around the body and in all cartilageus structures. The SheFurrs possess all bodily systems: nervous, circulatory, muscular, skeletal and cartilaginous structures, as well as internal organs. SheFurrs have a pulse that goes from the heart through the entire blood system throughout the body - the pulse is single, not double like human's.

Blood :

Orange. The blood have weak orange biolumen. The blood is slightlly warm so SheFurrs can be called a warm-blooded. SheFurr's blood is dangerous to other species and even to other SheFurrs from other hives / colonies - with other genetics. The blood is chemical-acidic and causes burning sensations as well as chemical burns when it comes into contact with biological tissue. Their blood is orange, highly efficient, and dangerous to other species.

Heart :

The heart is large and shaped like a thick, slightlly hexagonal disk (Queens have bigger heart). Color orange. It has six major vessels and numerous smaller tubes connected to it. The heart is wrapped in a thin transparent weaky-brownie membrane and located in the center of the heart-scar on chest. This structure - allows SheFurrs to survive extreme environmental conditions and process chemicals and acids at levels far beyond most species. Their blood is orange, highly efficient, and toxic to other species.

Lungs :

They have two symmetrical lungs, dark-orange in colors, shaped like thick crescent forms, three internal membrane layers, dense filtration structures (Queens have bigger lungs). Capable of breaking down harmful impurities, can extract breathable compounds from liquids instead of oxygen, nasal and throat membranes prevent water intake & liquids. They can breathe in chemically contaminated environments and underwater chemical mixtures. SheFurrs don't need oxygen - they need their home world's - SheWorld's atmosphere that is extremly chemical and acidic.

Digestive System :

The stomach is triple-chambered. Upper chamber processes organic matter. Middle chamber breaks down complex elements. Lower chamber final processing and extraction. All chambers are orange-green in colorations. SheFurrs can easily digest: bones, cartilages, keratin, and other complex substances of any biological origin.

Intestines are medium-length, slightly coiled, orange with reddish interior.

Feces are: spherical - but irregular, wrapped in a thin membrane, strongly scented with pheromonal / hormonal and fungal notes, contains fungal spores used for cultivation, not foul-smelling. Colors of feces are fungal whitish or weak-grays. All SheFurrs, regardless of castes even the royal ones (Not queen) - urinate and excrete feces through the same anatomical place, but not in same time - so SheFurrs can control this for their own choice what to excrete. SheFurrs don't need to excrete just a feces, their system is about to create special symbiotic-feces for fungal farms, so they need only to urinate.

Urine is: fluisy - but slightlly slimy, has whity color and very transparent, has a very strong peromonal and hormonal smells and can be used for territorial marking or chemical marks.

Toxin & Filtration Organ :

Toxin Filter - A spring-shaped organ with yellow-orange colorations and bright internal bubbles. A porous membrane cylinder runs through it's center. It neutralizes internal toxins and prevents self-poisoning, very effective.

Venom Gland :

A small dark-violet organ similar in shape to a compact liver. Produces paralytic venom used in saliva and in the tail stinger. Connected to mouth & tongue, and to tail stinger.

Tail stinger - At the tip of the tail is a sharp cartilaged stinger with venom. Contains significantly more venom than than in the mouth & tongue.

Pheromonal & Hormonal Organ :

A spherical organ covered in crater-like pores and micro-bubbles. Color: orange-yellow. Directly connected to hormonal and systemic pathways. Controls: communication, hive / colony coordination, hive / colony status signature / chemical signatures, reproductive organs & systems (Depends on caste), excrete & digestive systems, and environmental signaling.

Mouth Pores & Spittering Gland :

All SheFur castes and youngers, have two pores in their mouths for regurgitating construction foam and the acid-chemical glands is also the same two pores in their mouth on the sides near cheekbones that can spit small needles with acid and other unpleasant substances, they spit very quickly, three from each gland, and very accurately at the target in a small volley, after which the organ goes into recharge and creates new needles, the process is not fast - but also not long. But the same pores they have during the larval stage, which of the larva produces silk, upon maturation - the pore changes it's function and the individual begins producing Foam instead. At Larvae stage SheFurrs use this mouth pores for produce larval silk, which of construction foam & larval silk are easily produced in the symbionts organ of the individuals. All of them extremely useful, but consumes a lot of energy - because of which the individual needs food more often. IMPORTANT - that the individuals can choose any of the glandings that they have in the pores quickly, larvae can choose only between silk gland for producing silk or spittering gland for defense, all other older individuals (Also the queen has) can choose only between spittering gland or foam gland.

Symbionts Organ :

An organ: resembling a hairy bacterium, dark in color, filled with chambers for different SheFur symbionts. Connected by many small tubes. Maintains internal microbial partners, essential for SheFurrs biology & culture, all symbionts are created by SheFurrs (Queens).

Symbionts :

All symbionts of the SheFur species are microorganisms living inside and on the body of the SheFur, as well as in the SheFur environment. The SheFur themselves created these symbionts. The SheFur symbionts are more like hive micro-symbionts rather than external symbionts. The SheFur symbionts have very large spectre of uses: from cleaning the body to producing some materials. Symbionts of the SheFurrs do not depend on climate or environment, but only on the SheFur themselves and their environment.

Genetic System :

Hive Genetic Core - A small, gray-white organ shaped like a compact "pie". Stores hive's and queen's genetic code. And stores genetic samples from prey or enemies. A SheFurrs only needs to lick or consume a target to acquire genetic samples. Later this genetic material is expelled as a nutrient-genetic slurry for the queen to analyze by trophallaxis.

Respiratory Reserve Organ :

A special air-storage sac that holds native SheWorld's atmospheric composition. Supports lungs when external air is unavailable. Short-term survival only - ~30-40 minutes (SheWorld time).

Reproductive Biology :

SheFurr's reproduction uses a multi-stage internal system. A reproductive chamber located above the primary reproductive tract, inside is a hexagonal embryonic matrix, where early development and egg-formation occurs, specialized egg-transport canal extends along the tail during reproductive cycles like termite queen's sack. Hormonal cycles exist, but differ from mammals, only early queens have a hormonal discharge phase non-blood pheromone-rich fluid that ends once the egg-matrix develops. ONLY princesses / queens / gamergates / and false queens - have reproductive organ, all other SheFurrs doesn't have reproductive organ. False queens / gamergates / princesses - have more female-like reproductive organ, except the queens - that have: elongated - clitoris-like tube with a rhomboid tip at end, and has a brighty orange biolumen.

Milk Glands :

All SheFurrs have 2 body milk glands for feeding the youngers at their chest. The milk is very nutritioned, tastes like: honey + something playful + has little warmy + and light fungal taste. They possess nutrient-producing chest glands of varying sizes, so that any SheFur can feed the youngers if needed. Queens does not have standard milk, they have royal-jelly for feeding ONLY princesses. Also SheFurrs have nipples.

Hydration :

SheFurrs absorb moisture directly through internal systems, from the atmosphere, and from chemical liquids. Their filtration organs make them nearly immune to contaminated fluids.

Hygiene & Tongue Use :

The tongue has multiple hygienic functions: cleaning themselves, cleaning hive members, maintaining biological systems, healing wounds (saliva has regenerative properties), and has some amounts of paralytic venom.

Gases & Expulsions :

SheFurrs do not release gases - because their biology allows them to fully process everything they consume that has any biological structure. All gases produced during digestion are simply converted into energy by the body. SheFurrs do not have any forms of expulsion like mucuses. Regurgitation exists - but it's not vomiting - it works only when the individual has eaten something it cannot digest, so it simply spits it back out. But the SheFurrs, however: possess tear glands in all 5 eyes and produce tears that are brighty-white and transparent - because tears are hormonal and pheromonal, they also have sweat glands across their bodies - not for removing wastes from the organism - but for releasing hormones and pheromones used for chemical signatures and communication, as well as for cooling the body during the perihelion summer.

Regeneration :

Hair / teeth / symbionts / milk / and claws - can be easily regenerated. Sleep is essential for regeneration and neural stability.

Abilities -

Hive Mind + Individuality :

SheFurrs possess a caste structure similar to social insects, but at the same time - retain individual personalities and hive-mind intelligence, all individuals of any castes have their personalities, even the queens. A SheFurr's caste determines: physical traits, abilities, instincts, social role, combat specialization, weaknesses, anatomy and biology. All individuals in the hive are functionally females, but not in a human biological sense, like social insects.

Sensory Abilities :

SheFurrs have extreme sensory abilities. Unparalleled smell and hearing, ability to sense through multiple non-human spectrums, skin detects micro-vibrations, antennae transmit and receive complex sensory data, tongue functions as a high-precision chemical sensor snake-like. Two main eyes see standard spectrums for humanity like: infrared, ultraviolet, light, colorations, and night-vision. Two smaller eyes perceive: chemical, pheromonal, and hormonal - spectrums & signatures. The central Romb-eye rarely opens (Except princesses & queens - always opened), but when it does - it can see: emotional spectrum, behavioral spectrum, essence depth, structural essence, relational spectrums, and non-human perceptual fields. This eye is extremely powerful and rarely used.

Sensitive :

SheFurrs have sensitive skin and can feel their own fur hairs, and they also have cartilage with light nerves (Spine cartilage is like spinal-brain). SheFurrs have especially sensitive areas of the body: the pads of the fingers and paws on both hands and feets, the antennaes on the head are extremely sensitive, the entire spine from the head to the tip of the tail, also the tongue is very sensitive place, and nose / ears / eyes / heart-scar / reproductive organ (In some specific castes).

Their fur and hair dry over time if it's wet, taking about 50 minutes (SheWorld time) to reach a stable normal state. Fur does not affect acclimatization and does not depends on the climate.

SheFur likes the native climate of their planet, but most importantly - SheFurrs adore affection and caress.

Aclimate :

SheFurrs withstand temperatures from 60°C to -60°C easy, beyond this threshold - damage begins. Their reaction to cold is: shivering, and their blood becomes warmer. Their reaction to heat is sweating and fatigue. During the winter aphelion - SheFurrs often stay close together and rub against each other, or they enter a state of anabiosis.

Eggs / Larvae - are more depended on climate. Queen is the only individual that not depends on climate or temperatures, she's can stand temperatures from 120°C to -120°C.

Anabiosis & Freezing :

All castes and all life stages can enter anabiosis during extreme cold or winter. All stages & castes don't die when extremally freezed - that's gives SheFurrs ability to survive in ice or iced-form. ONLY queens still controling the hives and don't have basic anabiosis. That does not means that all SheFurrs really will be in anabiosis in their hives - because the hives have isolation and temperature regulation.

Swimming Ability :

All SheFurrs of any castes - are swimmers and can move through SheWorld's chemical slurries.

Shock-Absorbing Pads :

Every adult SheFur individual of any caste - has safety pads on their limbs. They works - like biological shock absorbers allowing safe landings from ~20 meters.

Pheromones & Hormones :

All castes & stages have their unique pheromon group - for example the warriors have their pheromones. And that works for all castes & stages. All castes & stages have the hormones, but reproductive castes like: queen / princess / gamergate / false queen mutation - have very strong and direct hormones.

Perception :

Smell: they sniff scents with their nose, and for them - smells are always more sweetish and chemical.

Taste: present, but weak - they feel the chemistry and depth of a product, as well as fungal tastes, dairy tastes, and sweet tastes, but all other tastes simply weaker for them.

Sound: they hear in familiar spectrums similar to bats and canine-furries.

Physical Durability :

Skeletal system is stronger than carbon-titanium composites, even a hammer cannot crack it easily. Bone armor protects the brain, cartilaged plates reinforce vital areas. Tail is multifunctional: movement, balance, combat, defense, and poison. Their body structure allows them to: climb extremely difficult surfaces and dimensions except very smooth ones, cling to walls and ceilings, move on two legs or shift to multi-point climbing stance. Naturally, SheFurrs are able to move their: ears, antennaes, and tail - as part of their: facial expressions, body language, and emotional display.

Movement & Combat :

Their movement style varies, but still have femininity. Graceful predator gait, extremely fast and agile bursts, silent stealth mode. SheFurrs are masters of combat, they absolutly dominate and adore the combat-arts, for addition - caste elites can use 6 arms in combat. They can: predict enemy weak points quickly, use: tai, claws, secondary arms & limbs, and jaws in coordinated attacks.

Perception Control :

SheFurrs can manually regulate their senses: reduce sound intensity to avoid deafening, dampen smell to avoid sensory overload, slow down sensory flow to avoid overwhelming input. Their perception can create a bullet-time effect - not actual time manipulation, but extreme neural processing speed.

Communication :

Young SheFurrs communicate via: pheromones, chemical, sounds. Older SheFurrs use hive language a complex mix of: resonance, pheromonal undertones, vocal patterns, body signals. Tail, ears, antennae - they all are a part of body language and can be used for this language.

Venom & Bite & Mouth Pores :

Bite causes mild weak bleeding. Saliva contains paralytic venom non-lethal weaky, but disabling. Tail stinger contains a stronger version of the same venom. Saliva also has healing and regenerative properties for wounds. All abilities and even mutations related to the mouth pores of the SheFurrs - are fully controlled by the individuals. This means that - the individual can simply choose a mode: if it wants to use foam, it selects that mode, and if it has the Spitter mutation - it can instead choose the needle-spitting mode, switching the properties of the pores back and forth as needed.

Hive Genetic Abilities :

They possess: genetic storage organ for hive and queen's DNA & predatory genetic acquisitioning - that stores genetic data from prey, ability to collect genetic material by licking or consuming a target, ability to deliver genetic slurry to the queen for analysis.

Trophallaxis :

SheFurrs can feed with foods and fluids each other like ants, regurgitating partially digested food to each other. It may seem disgusting, but in reality it follows the natural logic of the SheFur and is useful in some situations.

Cognitive Abilities :

3D hexagonal brain structure, extremely fast processing, predictive combat logic, hive-mind + individuality, ability to read environmental patterns instantly. SheFurrs cognitive abilities are often characterized by a multitaskining - meaning they can handle many small tasks at once or several complex tasks - because their brain is whole structure and their sensory system is extremly powerful.

Electricity :

SheFurrs have a higher pain threshold and can withstand greater amounts of electricity as damage. However, electricity can still injure or kill SheFur like any other living creature. SheFurrs can endure voltages up to approximately ~800-1200 V, at which serious injuries become likely, and anything above that is generally lethal. It is important to note that the exact threshold depends on: environment, conditions, weather, and other factors. On average, a SheFur can easily withstand voltages up to around 600 V.

Genetic -

Overview :

Genetics in SheFurr's biology is entirely controlled by the Queen. Genes are transmitted directly from the Queen into the eggs. After individuals mature - they can update their genetics through a special chemical-psyonic reaction initiated by the Queen inside the hive, it's developed to save the genome of an hive and to update the entire hive. Genetics is the Queen's primary survival tool, she shapes her hive's evolution.

Queen's Genetic Authority :

The Queen has full control over: the entire genome of the hive, genetic filtering, genetic editing, mutation suppression, mutation encouragement, and long-term evolutionary direction. She is essentially: the hive's chief neurobiologist, the hive's chief genetic engineer, and the main science of hive. Her genetic control is absolute.

Caste Independence :

Caste formation is controlled by: hive needs, genome, psycho-chemical signals, hive factors, Queen's directives. This was established earlier and remains a core rule.

Genome Compatibility :

SheFurrs are highly dependent on their genomes. Their social behavior within hives / colonies - is based on genetic recognition, the genome determines whether another SheFur is identified as a sister or as an unknown individual. This genomic dependency also determines compatibility between SheFurrs - from venom to weaponry. Anything that does not match the genome will not be compatible with an individual from outside.

Unique Genetic Lineages :

Every hive Queen has her own unique genetic lineage ranging from: extremely strong to intentionally mutated or to experimental. This is essential for survival, if the genome never changes - evolution stops and the species becomes obsolete. Some Queens deliberately introduce mutations to: study them, test them, adapt faster, strengthen the hive. Mutation probability is normally low - but controlled mutation is a strategic tool.

Core Principle of Evolution :

The SheFurr's evolutionary philosophy is simple: Adaptation & Pragmatism. The nor adapted will not survive. This is not ideology, it is the result of their evolutionary history. Their SheWorld forced them into this logic.

Biological Advantages :

SheFurr's genetics grants them: strong resistance to diseases and viruses, powerful immune systems, high toxin and venom resistance, advanced regeneration not overpowered - but strong, controlled mutation pathways - but not fully, and symbionts management. Everything in the hive, every caste, every organ, every behavior is encoded in their genome. Queens also control the symbionts living within the hive - meaning is symbionts that SheFur have in their body / hives.

Gene-Engineering Organ :

Inside every Queen is an organ directly connected to her brain near heart - the Queen's Gene-Engineering Organ. It allows her to: edit genes, remove unwanted traits, create new traits slowly, stabilize the hive's genome, and update the genome.

Time Required for Genetic Engineering :

Genetic engineering is not instant and not a cheat. It is: long, difficult, energy-intensive.

Typical timeframes: ~2-5 weaks / days (SheWorld time) - for standard genetic adjustments. ~2-3 months (SheWorld time) for very complex changes.

Accelerating and speed-ups only in extreme emergencies or real need. This keeps the system balanced and prevents overpowered evolution.

Environmental and Cultural Limits :

SheFurrs have a built-in limitations. They only evolve in response to threats. If left alone - they remain peaceful and do not escalate genetically. Acting like reagents (Reactionals). Their world and their cultural-understanding is designed to slow progress, prevent runaway evolution, maintain ecological balance. This is why their genetic engineering is powerful, but not world-breaking.

SheFurrs with such a genetic system - could have long since advanced in development, but they do not do this purely on principle - because they are honest and want to understand all their surroundings themselves before making genetic changes. That is - SheFurrs don't rush in evolutions and technologies, but they are reactionaries.

But all of this can be circumvented in different ways.

Evolutionary Victory :

Humanity won evolution through brain. SheFur won evolution through: superior brain structure, development of the gene-engineering organ, enhanced perception, controlled mutation, resonance.

Mutations (Permitted) -

Naturally, one mutation does not cancel another, meaning - a single individual may have more than one mutation, but this increases the rarity by 2 times, and the more mutations the same individual has, the greater the rarity - meaning if there are 6 mutations then, it is 6 times rarer. Mutations in SheFur do occur by themselves - but rarely, and should expect that almost the entire hive / colony will - consist of ordinary individuals.

1. Freckles -

An unusual appearance mutation in which regardless of caste - darker or whitish-dark colored freckles appear on a SheFurr's face, the mutation affects only appearance and nothing more.

2. Weak Antennae -

A very rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur almost do not perceive resonance, such individuals may be more self-sufficient and less dependent on their surroundings, but they have problems with sociality, the mutation is rare and genetically permitted because sometimes more independent individuals are needed in the hive + it does not affect hive logic in any way and is considered safe.

3. Predatorochromia -

A very rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur develops predatory pupils like those of cats and wolves instead of the typical SheFur ones + they can contract and expand like those of terrestrial predators, the mutation is very rare and affects only appearance, and also slightly improves visual focus.

4. Venairs -

A very rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur develops hexagonal pupils in all eyes + they can contract and expand, but in a spiral manner like target-locking, the mutation is very rare and affects only appearance, and also greatly improves concentration and visual focusing + the pupils become more crystallized - They protect the eyes better from dust.

5. False Queen -

An extremely rare mutation that is not present in all hive genetic branches, in which a SheFur individual regardless of caste - may have a functional fertilization organ like that of a gamergate's or a princesse's, and it is operational, a very rare and quite dangerous mutation for hive logic, but fortunately this false fertilization organ will never replace real queens and is rather a mutation of appearance in which the individual develops a reproductive organ, but still there is a danger to the royals though a small one, since a false queen usually is nowhere near a real queen in mind and logic and cannot easily control the hive or replace the queen, the mutation remains in some hive genetic branches - because it can actually be very useful in the most extreme cases when: the queen, princesses, and gamergates die.

6. Sweetie -

An unusual mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur develops a wild fondness for sweet things, the mutation is more of an individual taste rather than a mutation, completely safe overall.

7. Pirie -

A very rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur has a pink warm striped coloration, but the orange biolumen areas become soft whity, the mutation is purely external and very rare, which makes it quite interesting and unique. (It has no relation to the Nurse caste since this caste originally may have any pink color palettes and patterns).

8. Kumie -

An incredibly rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur has purple and orange colors in stripes, but the orange biolumen areas do not change, the mutation is purely external and incredibly rare, which makes it almost singular.

9. Hellvie -

An incredibly rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur has a red striped coloration, but the orange biolumen areas become yellow in color, the mutation is purely external and incredibly rare which makes it almost singular.

10. Bleakness -

A rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur has white biolumen instead of the orange biolumen areas, a purely external mutation (Has no relation to the pink mutation).

11. Fatty Milk Gland -

A very rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur has, instead of normal liquid milk, a very viscous slightly fermented milk like creamy-milk inside the milk gland, the mutation is rare and not critical, but not safe since such milk can harden inside the gland like stones that rot, which may cause pain and burning inside, as a plus this milk is more nutritious and even healthier than normal for the Youngers.

12. Sweet Milk Gland -

A rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur has sweeter, more energetic milk inside the milk gland, the mutation is rare and almost safe, the only possible issue being slight burning inside and outside, as a plus this milk is much tastier and more energetic.

13. Hyper Milk Gland -

A rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur has excessively frequent formation and release of milk, but in two variants - either too much milk forms inside and may cause pain as well as heaviness and sometimes the milk needs to be drained, or on the contrary, too much milk is released because of which the milk often flows at any touch, causing the size of the milk gland to decrease significantly, in any case the mutation is not critical, but also not pleasant, yet it allows feeding more young and forming more milk.

14. Spotlings -

An unusual mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur has a pattern of spots instead of stripes, the mutation is purely external.

15. Resonance-sensory Madness -

A rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur has everything in the brain mixed together, but not as usual into a unified structure, but instead into a heap of unrelated information and abstractions, a very unpleasant mutation in which there may be headaches, frequent overheating of the sensory system and resonance, also hallucinations and weakened sociality, the mutation exists only because such an individual may accidentally notice and connect something that was not there at all or something interesting that needs to be studied.

16. Autothysis -

A rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur, but often specifically in combat castes, has the stomach system and the gene-code organ explode after death when intruders appear nearby (The explosion is not strong, it's weaky), dispersing a dangerous chemical-acidic mist or liquid, the mutation is purely defensive, but carries danger because autothysis is controlled by body chemistry and not by the individual.

17. Silk Gland Mutation -

A rare mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur retains the silk-producing organ from the larval stage, which is extremely useful not only as a resource, but also as a defensive spit, since such a stream instantly hardens like sticky webbing, the mutation is very useful but consumes a lot of energy, because of which the individual needs food more often.

18. Rage -

An unusual mutation in which regardless of caste - SheFur under certain factors such as combat situations or stress or anger falls into Rage, in which concentration on combat increases and the individual becomes more than itself - meaning it feels a surge of strength, but this is an effective placebo, and reaction speed increases due to increased adrenaline in the blood, it has side effects in the form of sudden fatigue after Rage and sometimes dulls emotionality in moments, the mutation seems useful - but has side effects.

19. Biofuel Organ -

A rare mutation in which, in any caste except the royal ones - SheFur develops from birth an additional organ with symbionts that digest certain materials so that they produce the standard biofuel of SheFurrs, such an individual simply needs to eat more and then regurgitate the biofuel, the mutation is useful, but dangerous because biofuel, when ignited, burns with a chemical-acidic flame that is extremely dangerous for all forms of life including SheFur, meaning that if the organ is hit unsuccessfully or damaged, the biofuel inside the individual may ignite and melt through the individual, therefore the mutation is rare but acceptable.

20. Reinforced Skin -

An experimental mutation that is being studied, in which regardless of caste - SheFurr's skin is capable of withstanding the chemical-acidic fire from SheFurrs own biofuel, the mutation is experimental and in the process of study, so it may turn out to be dangerous or critically defective and therefore is almost singular.

Main Weaknesses -

Needs - Sleep / Rest / Eat / Calm / Waste :

SheFurrs require all of this like all other organisms. They sleep 1-5 hours (SheWorld time). Even during sleep they remain partially aware of surroundings, sleep is a full neural rest not a processing state. They do not think or work during sleep - it is pure recovery and Archiving the individual's life experience to the Archive. SheFurrs are omnivores, but in a very broad biological sense. They eat: fungus grown inside their hives, meat, any organic matter, anything that has organic structures or biological structures. IMPORTANT - that ONLY queens don't really need all of this, even calm.

Antennae Vulnerability :

Their antennae are directly connected to the brain. Damage to an antennae: disrupts communication, destabilizes sensory processing, causes partial neural shocks, does not kills the individual - but severely impairs function. This is one of their most critical biological weak points.

Atmospheric Dependency :

SheFurrs require the atmosphere of SheWorld to function at full capacity. Their native air is: toxic, spore-dense, chemically aggressive. They can survive outside it, but: sensory systems overloads faster, filtration organs works harder, and performance decreases.

Sensory Overload :

Their senses are extremely powerful - but this also means: extreme noises, extreme lights, extreme chemical saturations, rapid environmental shifts - can overload their sensory system. They can regulate perception, but not infinitely. That depends by how many individuals of SheFurr's they have - because when they have numbers they can react like hive-mind that means - SheFurrs in big groups cannot be easily overloaded. Queens don't have overloads, they are the hive's / colonie's neural core.

Central Eye Romb :

The Romb-eye is extremely useful, but highly vulnerable. Direct impact or targeted attack can: blind the eye, disrupt deepy emotional spectrums of perception, and destabilize resonance. It is rarely opened for this reason.

Heart-Scar Vulnerability :

The heart-scar on the chest is easily protected - but precise, direct strike with enough force at the correct angle - can damage the heart. This is difficultly, but possible.

Venom Compatibility :

Their venom is safe only for individuals from the same hive / colony / or with the same genome. If two SheFurrs from different hives or genetic lines bite each other - their venoms affect each other normaly. This creates natural boundaries between hives / colonies.

Pain & Damage :

Any damage can activate pain, SheFurr's pain is more slightlly and simple than human pain. But damaging very sensitive parts of SheFurr's body, will be painful. Tongue, ears, nose, fingertips of fingers & paws, tail, antennae, eyes, heart-scar. All of this are sensitive places, this places when damaged can be critical and painful.
