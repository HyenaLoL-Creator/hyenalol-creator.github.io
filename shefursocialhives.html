Hive - Main -

Overview :

SheFur hives / colonies - are not a living organisms. It's a structures builded by the SheFurrs. Most creatures in the ecosystem fear the hives / colonies - which is logical given it's scale and nature.

Construction :

Most of the hives / colonies - are built from the outside inward using a single primary material produced by the SheFurrs themselve, although it's not the only construction material - just the main one used for hives / colonies. Adult SheFur individuals, using their symbiont organ - can produce the main building material without any special requirements, namely - SheFurr's Construction Foam. With this foam, most of the hives / colonies - are built by regurgitating it through special pores in the individuals mouth. The foam is named so - because it looks like a very airy, frothy substance - but not for long: it quickly hardens within 2 hours, gaining strength while remaining elastic to protect against earthquakes and to increase overall structural efficiency. The foam itself appears as a gray-white-dark mixture of colors, and after hardening it becomes part of the hive / colony structures, taking on a darker shade. The material is called foam precisely - because the individuals can easily shape and mold it however they need, using the foam itself.
